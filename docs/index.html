Features

Everything your server can do, in your pocket.

Halftone talks straight to your server. Playlists, stars and play counts stay in sync with the web interface you already use.

Palette · per cover

Colours from every cover

Buttons, gradients and the player pick up the tones of the album that's playing and fade over when the track changes.

Original files

Offline listening

Download a song, album or playlist. You get the original file, with its cover, and it plays locally even when you're online.

MP3 · AAC · FLAC · Opus

Gapless playback

The next track is ready before this one ends, so live albums and DJ mixes flow on without a click or a pause.

Synced both ways

Playlists

Create, rename and fill playlists on the go. Add a whole album in two taps. Changes land on your server at once.

Songs · albums · artists

Favourites

Heart anything, including the song that's playing. Your favourites have their own tab and show up in every list.

½ track or 4 min

Scrobbling

Plays are counted the standard way, so Recently played and Most played are right. Navidrome can pass them on to Last.fm or ListenBrainz.

128 · 192 · 320 · original

Streaming quality

Pick a bitrate for mobile data, or stream the untouched original at home. Downloads are always the original.

Media session

Plays in the background

Controls in your notifications, on the lock screen, on Bluetooth headphones and in Android Auto. A call or a navigation prompt pauses it for you.

Why Halftone

You built your own music server. Your player should be as good.

Most Subsonic clients get the job done. Halftone is for people who also care how it feels to scroll through their library at night.

Designed, not just functional

Big covers, real typography, soft motion and a player that changes colour with your music. It is the reason this app exists.

Nothing in between

Halftone connects only to your server. There is no Halftone account, no analytics and no ads. Your password stays on your phone and is sent as a salted token.

Respects your files

Gapless playback, original-quality downloads and a quality switch for when you're on mobile data. Your FLACs stay FLACs.

Your server stays in charge

Playlists, favourites and play counts live on the server, so the web interface, your desktop client and Halftone always agree.

    Download

    Get Halftone on your phone and desktop.

    Install it, type your server address and sign in with your usual username and password. Your library appears straight away.

    Questions

    Good to know

    Do I need my own server?

    Yes. Halftone plays music from a server you run yourself, such as Navidrome on a home server, NAS or VPS. It has no music catalogue of its own.

    Does it work without internet?

    Songs, albums and playlists you've downloaded play anywhere. If the app can't reach your server, it offers your downloads right away.

    Where is my password stored?

    Only on your own device. Each request to your server uses a salted hash instead of the password itself.

    Why does every screen look different?

    Halftone reads the colours of the cover that's playing and builds the theme from them, with checks so text always stays readable.

    Will my plays show up on Last.fm?

    Halftone reports plays to your server. Connect Last.fm or ListenBrainz in Navidrome and it forwards them for you. You can turn scrobbling off in Settings.

    Does it work with Android Auto?

    Yes. Browse your home screen, playlists, favourites, artists and downloads on the car's screen, search, or just say "Hey Google, play … on Halftone". Installed the APK yourself? Turn on Unknown sources in Android Auto's developer settings first.

    Is there a desktop version?

    Yes, for Linux. It has a sidebar, a player bar and a queue panel, gapless playback through libmpv, and works with your media keys and the GNOME or KDE media controls. Windows and macOS aren't available yet.

    Is there an iPhone version?

    Not right now. Halftone runs on Android and Linux. Suggest it above if you'd use it.
